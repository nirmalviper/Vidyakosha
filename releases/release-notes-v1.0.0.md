## Highlights
1. **Continuous Multilingual E-Reader & Top-3 Language Selector**
   - 48+ global & classical languages supported with a Top-3 Preferred Languages filter for instant switching and reduced backend load.
   - Progressive infinite-loading book reading canvas with continuous TTS audio read-aloud across sections.
2. **Client-Side P2P Extraction Studio (PDF, EPUB, TXT, Camera OCR)**
   - Extracts paragraphs on-device from user-owned PDFs, EPUBs, TXT files, and camera scans without uploading restricted source files.
3. **Permanent Versioned Translation Repository**
   - Stores and versions every contributed or AI-generated translation with full model/contributor provenance.
4. **Superadmin Backend Console & Entitlements Engine**
   - Protected `superadmin` role (`superadmin@lipi.org`), dynamic database-backed commercial plans & pricing, granular user & book entitlements, metadata registry, and immutable `admin_audit_logs`.
